02 / THE STORY

Nocturne's History

The Question

Nocturne’s streaming page began with a simple question: what makes a stream worth watching? We started by looking into what streaming actually meant beyond simply watching someone play a game, exploring different forms of content such as gaming livestreams, casual conversations, music, special events, and other interactive experiences.

Finding Our Audience

We also looked at the platforms where this kind of content thrives, eventually focusing on YouTube because of its accessibility and its popularity among younger audiences. This was supported by Ofcom’s research on children and young people’s online habits, which found that 58% of children aged 3–17 watched livestreamed video content, increasing to 80% among 16–17-year-olds. YouTube was also used by 88% of children aged 3–17 in the study.

Building Nocturne

From there, we shaped Nocturne around the people we actually wanted to reach: students and teens between the ages of 13 and 18. We wanted our content to feel familiar and accessible, but still give viewers something more than simply watching people play and bicker.


Gaming became the natural direction, particularly games that our audience could easily access, such as Roblox and Minecraft. We wanted our streams to balance entertainment with something viewers could take away from them, whether that meant discovering a new strategy, learning a maneuver, or simply enjoying the chaos of watching friends compete.

Pro vs. Noob

This eventually became the idea behind our first stream: a “Pro vs. Noob” challenge, where our nine-member team would split into experienced and beginner players.


The contrast between the two groups gives the stream its competitive and comedic side while also allowing viewers to see different approaches to the game.

More Than A Stream

But Nocturne was never meant to exist only on the screen during a livestream. We wanted it to feel like a space that viewers could return to, which led us to create a webpage that acts as the home of the project.


While YouTube serves as the main stage for our streams and uploaded gameplay, the website brings everything else together—from behind-the-scenes content and teasers to information about our team, upcoming schedules, and a community space for announcements and interaction.

Still In Progress

Nocturne is still a work in progress, but that is part of its history: it started with research, grew from an understanding of its audience, and became a streaming experience built around the things we wanted gaming content to be: entertaining, interactive, accessible, and very distinctly ours.

03 / BEHIND THE STREAM

Stream Info

Everything behind the screen.

Where To Watch

Nocturne’s main stage is YouTube, where our gaming streams and recorded content will be hosted. We chose YouTube because it is accessible to our target audience and allows us to combine livestreaming with uploaded videos that viewers can return to later.


Our Nocturne website works alongside the stream as the central hub for everything else. Here, viewers can find our streaming schedule, team information, announcements, teasers, behind-the-scenes content, and other exclusive material.

The Setup

Creating a stream takes more than simply pressing that “Go Live” button. The Nocturne team uses a combination of cameras, microphones, computers, lighting, and streaming software to capture and present our gameplay.


Since Nocturne is a work in progress, part of our process involves testing our setup and making adjustments along the way. We experiment with camera placement, lighting, audio levels, and our streaming tools.

How Long Is A Stream?

Our streams are planned to run for approximately 90 minutes, depending on the challenge, gameplay, and how the session develops.


For our first stream, the main event will be our “Pro vs. Noob” challenge, where our nine-member team will be divided between experienced and beginner players.


The stream will follow the challenge from beginning to end, giving viewers a chance to see the competition, the strategies, and—inevitably—the chaos that comes with putting nine people into one game.

What To Expect

Every Nocturne stream is built around three things: gameplay, interaction, and entertainment.


Viewers can expect challenges, competition, team interactions, unexpected moments, and opportunities to learn from the different approaches our players take.


As Nocturne develops, our streaming format may evolve with it. New games, challenges, and types of content can be introduced based on what works best for our audience. The goal isn't simply to go live—it is to create a stream that feels worth coming back to.
